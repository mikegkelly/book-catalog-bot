How it works

Three steps from a messy shelf to a full catalog.

Answer a few setup questions

Which app you use (BookBuddy, Goodreads, LibraryThing or a spreadsheet), whether you want values and a library page, any extras, and how you name your shelves.

Send a shelf photo

One shelf or stack at a time. Say where it is, like "Office, Shelf 3". If a spine is hard to read, it asks instead of guessing.

Get your catalog

Every book with edition, ISBN, summary, value and its spot on the shelf, plus a ready-to-import file for your app and an updated library page.

What it does

Everything below comes with the template. Extras are off until you turn them on.

📷

Photo to catalog

Reads every title and author in a shelf or stack photo and matches the exact edition and ISBN, with publisher, year, format, page count and a short summary.

💲

Values

Used and new estimates from eBay sold listings, AbeBooks and Amazon, each with its source and date. Flags possible first editions, signed copies and collectible publishers.

📍

Shelf location

Numbers books left to right so you can find them on the real shelf: Office, Shelf 3, #7. A new photo of the same shelf updates the numbers.

🔁

Duplicates check

Spots books you might already own, by ISBN or title and author, and asks you before merging anything.

📤

Exports

Import files for BookBuddy, Goodreads or LibraryThing, or a plain spreadsheet, with step-by-step import instructions.

🔎

Library page

One searchable page of your whole collection with covers, filters by author, genre, location and value, and a shelf view.

🏷️

eBay listing drafts

Title, condition, item details, description, photo list, a price from recent sold listings and a shipping tip. You paste it in and post it yourself.

💭

Find a book

Describe a half-remembered plot or quote and get ranked guesses, with why each one fits and where it sits if you already own it.

🛒

Find copies

Legal copies to buy, borrow from your library (Libby, Hoopla) or read free from the public domain, with links and prices.

✨

Optional extras

Wish-list price alerts, a quarterly value refresh, a lending tracker and a cloud backup. Pick the ones you want during setup.

📸 Photo tips

  • Send full-resolution photos, not screenshots or compressed copies.
  • One shelf or stack at a time, spines straight and well lit.
  • Say where the shelf is when you send it, like "Office, Shelf 3".
  • For old, signed or possibly valuable books, add the title page and copyright page. That's how editions are confirmed.

🛑 What it never does

  • Never buys, sells, lists, posts or contacts anyone without your OK.
  • Never presents a guess as a fact: values are estimates with a source and date, and unclear books get a question, not a guess.
  • Only points you to legal copies, never pirated ones.
  • Never merges or deletes catalog entries without asking.
